# Online Courses & Certifications

This section documents online courses, certifications, and independent learning activities undertaken to strengthen my technical knowledge and professional development.

## Learning Records

For each course or learning programme, record:

- **Course Name:** Official course title.
- **Platform:** Where the course is offered.
- **Provider/Instructor:** Course provider or instructor, if available.
- **Start Date:** When learning began.
- **Completion Date:** When completed, if applicable.
- **Status:** Not Started, In Progress, or Completed.
- **Skills Developed:** Technologies, concepts, and practical abilities gained.
- **Key Takeaways:** Important lessons and knowledge acquired.
- **Practical Application:** Exercises, assignments, or projects completed.

## Learning Principles

- Prioritise practical knowledge over simply collecting certificates.
- Complete exercises and assignments where available.
- Record meaningful progress and challenges.
- Apply newly acquired skills to real projects.
- Keep learning records accurate and up to date.
